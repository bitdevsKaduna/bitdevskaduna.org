### Agenda & Topics

This month's BitDevs meetup features two technical sessions exploring recent developments in Bitcoin infrastructure, node operation, and protocol-level improvements:

- **Secure Fountain Coded Pruned Nodes**  
  Exploring how fountain codes can be used to improve how pruned Bitcoin nodes obtain and recover blockchain data while reducing storage and bandwidth requirements. We will cover underlying concepts, secure fountain coding mechanics, and implications for node resilience.

- **Coldcard & BIP110 Incidents Breakdown**  
  Taking a closer look at Coldcard hardware wallet vulnerabilities and the technical discussions surrounding BIP110, exploring how hardware wallets fit into the broader Bitcoin security and self-custody ecosystem.

- **Networking & Open Discussion Session**
